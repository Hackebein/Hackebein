Hi, I'm Lars (Hackebein), a developer and engineer. I spent more than a decade building and running e-commerce platforms. For the past year, I've worked on software development and DevOps for [SINA Cloud](https://www.secunet.cloud/en/services/sina-cloud), a platform for running applications securely. Outside work, I design hardware and write software for things I do in VR.

## Things I work on

### VPMM

I built VPMM (VPM Manager) to make it easier to find and manage VRChat creator packages. You can browse packages, compare versions and Unity test results, and add listings to Creator Companion or ALCOM.

- [Browse VPMM](https://vpmm.dev/)
- [VPM Packager for Unity](https://github.com/Hackebein/hackebein.vpm.packager)

### VRChat Wiki

I've helped maintain the [VRChat Wiki](https://wiki.vrchat.com/) since it started. It's written by the community and hosted on vrchat.com. I tend to write about whatever I find that could use a page or a better explanation. That's taken me from OSC and avatar features to community tools, events, and upcoming features. I also translate wiki pages into German and write bots that keep things like world stats and client versions up to date. My [edit history](https://wiki.vrchat.com/wiki/Special:Contributions/Usr_40be82e7-f7d5-4548-b314-3fc7b26d22ee) shows the range.

### VaOBS

VaOBS is an OBS plugin that lets you record the VRChat window with just the video player's audio, synced to the picture.

- [VaOBS source and releases](https://github.com/Hackebein/VaOBS)

### ANT+ OSC

Peep and I built a Windows app that brings live sensor readings, like heart rate and cycling cadence, into VRChat and OBS. It supports ANT+ today. We plan to add Bluetooth Low Energy and are looking at devices that use their own Bluetooth protocols.

### DoKomi

I've been involved with DoKomi, an anime and Japan convention in Düsseldorf, since 2013. I started with the Retro Arcade cabinets and later helped at the entrances and info desks. Currently core team of logistics and managing the VIP area. The convention has grown from a small (~500 visitors) local event to something much larger (~200k visitors), and it still feels like the same friendly environment.
